About me

A life in education

The boardroom, the classroom and the family dinner table. Balancing all three is an art, and it has taught me my most valuable lessons.

As a mother of three, my children are my daily reminder of why the work we do in education matters so deeply. They keep me grounded, curious and constantly learning. Professionally, my journey as a school owner and director has been shaped by a deep commitment to academic excellence, moral grounding and compassionate leadership.

I founded Cheldem International School in Benin with a vision to build a holistic learning environment where children can discover their potential from early years through basic education. Over the years, that vision has grown into a broader passion: mentoring others.

I love partnering with women, school administrators and young educators who are building meaningful careers and institutions without losing sight of what matters most at home.
